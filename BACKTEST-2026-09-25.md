# Backtest: "copy the leaderboard whales" (run 2026-09-25, script `scripts/backtest-whales.ts`)

**Method.** For each leaderboard trader: pull up to 1,500 most recent trades from `data-api.polymarket.com/trades`, group by market+outcome, resolve every market through `clob.polymarket.com/markets/<conditionId>` (`tokens[].winner`), and compute the P/L of copying every BUY at the whale's own fill price and holding to resolution (`copyPnl`, `copyROI`). `whaleNet` is the whale's own realized P/L on the same resolved markets (sells counted). 5-minute/15-minute crypto up-or-down scalps excluded. 8,992 markets resolved.

**Cohorts.** A = top 15 by 30-day P/L (selected on the outcome measured → survivorship-biased). B = top 15 by all-time volume (selected on activity, not outcome).

| Cohort | traders | resolved positions | copy win-rate | copy staked | copy P/L | **copy ROI** | whales' own net |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A: top 30d P/L | 15 | 6,142 | 49.9 % | $74.0M | +$1.23M | **+1.7 %** | +$1.86M |
| B: top all-time volume | 15 | 6,880 | 54.7 % | $53.9M | −$0.43M | **−0.8 %** | −$0.08M |

Per-trader spread inside cohort A: −8.8 % (maz26) to +41 % (Herdonia, 106 resolved, 100 % sports). Sports are 50–100 % of every whale's book.

**Reading.**
1. Blind copying of "top" wallets has no edge: +1.7 % in the biased cohort, −0.8 % in the unbiased one, before your own slippage and the price impact of copying.
2. The leaderboard rewards volume in sports handicaps and totals, where whales are ~50/50 pickers on large size. Their P/L is variance, not calibration.
3. The spread between wallets (−12 % to +41 %) is where a product can live: score by calibrated ROI **per category** with significance tests, weight recent windows, and only follow above-normal size from wallets that pass. That is Phase 2 (`lib/intel/wallet-score.ts`).
4. Never compute win rates from `/positions`: winners get redeemed and vanish, losers stay at $0. A first draft of this backtest did that and reported 0 % win rates.

**Caveats.** No leaderboard history exists, so cohort A is in-sample by construction. Copy fills assume the whale's price with zero slippage. Trades capped at 1,500 per wallet (newest first), so heavy traders are measured on their last ~2 weeks.
